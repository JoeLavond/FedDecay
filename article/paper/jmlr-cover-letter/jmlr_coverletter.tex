Dear Editors:

We are writing to submit our manuscript ``\red{FedDecay: Adapting to Data Heterogeneity in Federated Learning with Within-Round Learning Rate Decay}'' to the \emph{Journal of Machine Learning Research}.

Our manuscript studies \red{the generalization ability of federated learning solutions in the presence of various local data diversity}, which is important because of \red{the inability to ever know any application's real degree of data diversity due to the data privacy requirements of federated learning}.
